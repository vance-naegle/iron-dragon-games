Privacy Policy

Magic Window  ·  Developed by Iron Dragon Games  ·  Last updated: August 11, 2026

Overview

Magic Window is a free browser-based augmented reality (AR) experience developed by Iron Dragon Games. This policy explains how we handle information when you use it.

Camera Access

Magic Window uses your device's camera to detect a printed marker image and render AR content anchored to it (via 8th Wall's WebAR image tracking). The camera feed is processed locally on your device to track the marker and is not recorded, stored, uploaded, or transmitted to us or any third party.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal details to use this experience.

Third-Party Services (8th Wall)

This experience uses the open-source 8th Wall Engine to enable camera-based image tracking. The engine runs entirely in your browser — there is no separate hosted 8th Wall account or service involved, and no camera data is sent to 8th Wall or its maintainers.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this and other websites.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out via the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

Cookies

We do not set cookies ourselves. Third-party partners (including Google and 8th Wall) may set cookies or use local storage on your device as described above. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

Third-Party Links

Ads displayed on this site may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
