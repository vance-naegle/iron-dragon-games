Reporting a bug

If a game is not behaving, a few details make it much easier to track down. Please include:

  • The name of the game (Bad Triangles, Breakout, Avoid, or an AR experience).
  • Your device and browser, for example "iPhone 14, Safari" or "Windows laptop, Chrome".
  • What you were doing when it happened, and what you expected to happen instead.
  • Whether the screen was in landscape or portrait, and whether your display is scaled above 100 percent.
  • A screenshot, if you can grab one.

AR experiences

The AR experiments need a phone with a rear camera and permission to use it. If one will not start, check that your browser is allowed to use the camera, that there is good lighting, and that you are on a recent version of Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). Tell me what happened and I will take a look.
